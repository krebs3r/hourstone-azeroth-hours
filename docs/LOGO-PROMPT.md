## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand.
Reference: use ONLY the Soundstone logo in the TOP LEFT of the supplied sheet as the original visual family. The other three sheet icons indicate the simple, bold gold material style; do not include them in the output.
Create ONE new Hourstone sibling logo: the same pale grey teleportation stone with its bright cyan-blue carved spiral rune and warm gold mounting pieces, now combined with ONE prominent golden HOURGLASS. Remove every sound-wave arc. No clock hands, clock arc, clock face or ticks anywhere.
Keep the stone's tilted upright silhouette, blue rune, chunky painted fantasy-item style and recognizable Soundstone color palette. Position the stone slightly left of center. Place a single upright hourglass in the right foreground, partly overlapping only the stone's lower-right edge. Leave the blue rune readable and unobscured. The hourglass should be LARGE and unmistakable, approximately two thirds of the stone's height and about 40 percent of the total icon width. Together they form one compact balanced emblem, not two disconnected objects.
Readability at 32 pixels is the main requirement. Make the hourglass a SIMPLE, BOLD golden fantasy UI symbol: broad horizontal top and bottom gold caps, two large tapered glass chambers meeting at a distinctly pinched narrow waist, one clear triangular amber-gold sand mound, and a very short simple sand stream. Strong dark-bronze edging and clean negative spaces make the familiar hourglass silhouette instantly readable. No ornate handles or side pillars, no gears, no filigree, no tiny sparkles, no fussy glass reflections. Use restrained beveled warm-gold metal shading matching the original Soundstone sound waves, with a limited number of large readable shapes.
The stone provides the family identity; the hourglass provides a clear time-tracking identity. Avoid a tiny badge that gets lost, avoid covering the blue rune, avoid an enclosing circular border.
Square logo composition, generous transparent margin, fills approximately 84 percent of the canvas. Genuinely transparent RGBA background, no checkerboard, no black or white rectangle. No letters, labels or extra icons.
