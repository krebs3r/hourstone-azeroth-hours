# Hourstone 0.4.2 — 2026-11-19

Hourstone has a new name that says what it does: **Hourstone – Azeroth Playtime
Tracker**. WoW's addon list, the window title, WoW: Forever's title strip and the
tooltips of the minimap button and the Addons menu show it. The addon folder, your
saved characters and settings, `/hourstone` and `/azerothhours` stay as they are;
nothing is migrated.

Everything else is as in
[Hourstone 0.4.1](https://github.com/krebs3r/hourstone-azeroth-hours/releases/tag/v0.4.1).
With WoW closed, extract `Hourstone-0.4.2.zip` into `Interface/AddOns`.

Automated checks and the in-game test are described in
[validation](https://github.com/krebs3r/hourstone-azeroth-hours/blob/v0.4.2/docs/VALIDATION.md).
