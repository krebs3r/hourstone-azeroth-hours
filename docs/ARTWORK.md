# Artwork provenance

**Approved direction:** the fourth Hourstone design, a grey teleport stone with a glowing blue rune and a golden foreground hourglass. User approval was received on 2026-09-15 before addon implementation.

`assets/Logo.png` is the approved Imagegen output (1254 × 1254, RGBA). It uses the original [Soundstone icon sheet](https://github.com/krebs3r/soundstone-azeroth-audio/blob/main/docs/icons-source.png) as its visual reference. No artistic edits were applied after generation. The clock arcs and hands from the previous three concepts were rejected; they are not shipped.

The generation prompt is preserved in [LOGO-PROMPT.md](LOGO-PROMPT.md). `tools/assets.py` mechanically resizes the PNG to 256 × 256 and writes an uncompressed 32-bit TGA with alpha for WoW.

`RetailPanel.png`, `ClassicPanel.png` and `HeaderClose.png` are copied from [Soundstone's docs/assets](https://github.com/krebs3r/soundstone-azeroth-audio/tree/main/docs/assets) and converted without resizing into TGA. Copyright © 2026 krebs3r, MIT; the license is included at the repository root and in the installable addon. Hourstone renders the frame as nine texture regions, preserving the source corners.

The approved [Mockup 05](mockup-05/index.html) and historical [Mockup 04](mockup/index.html) are retained as self-contained previews. Mockup 05 uses Georgia to approximate the native WoW standard font. Data is fictional; browser rendering does not prove in-game fidelity.

Mockup 05 / test 3 also use original Soundstone `Toggle`, `ToggleRed`, `Rivet`, `GoldThumb` and `SilverThumb` artwork, pinned to commit `85290d29c3de18f8612d679b093e509e6725d890`. Source PNG bytes and Git blob hashes are in `mockup-05/soundstone-assets.json`; every converted TGA pixel is checked against the source. Panel corners render at 7px Retail / 10px Classic; button corners at 4px. The actual addon reads the client-owned `Interface\WorldMap\GEAR_64GREY` texture, which is not redistributed.

Code-defined control backgrounds, triangles, grip dots, checkbox tick, gear, pink heart, minimap circle and mask are generated by tools/assets.py. The approved stone/hourglass artwork is unchanged; the minimap displays the whole texture at 18 × 18 inside a 28 × 28 button, with a 24-unit circular mask. Nontransparent artwork stays at least 2 UI units inside the rim; the button inherits the minimap scale. `Heart.tga` is a 32px power-of-two texture rendered in a 9 × 9px region, in #A75466. Version, heart and author are separated by 5px gaps. Font licenses are documented in FONTS.md. Starting with test 5, the settings checkbox uses the client-owned UICheckButtonTemplate and its checked/pressed/hover artwork; the old generated tick is retained for historical and approximate browser previews only.
